Made for this game
------------------

"Dune riding" (audio/dune-riding.mp3), the music on the bike, is by Veikko Suhonen, made for dadajam.
"Desert ambience" (audio/desert-ambience.mp3), the music outdoors, is by Veikko Suhonen, made for dadajam.
"Death" (audio/death.mp3), the music when you die, is by Veikko Suhonen, made for dadajam.
"Epic battle" (audio/epic-battle.mp3), the music in a fight with a boss or a guardian up close, is by Veikko Suhonen, made for dadajam.

All other models, textures, icons and sounds were made for dadajam: models and icons are generated
in Blender by the scripts in tools/ of the source repository, and the wind and the crash-site electronics are synthesised in code.
